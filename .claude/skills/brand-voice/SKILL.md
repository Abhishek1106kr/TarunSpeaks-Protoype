# Brand voice — TarunSpeaks and Tarun Makhija

Built only from verified first-party material (listed at the end). Never invent an anecdote, a quote, a belief or a habit. If a line needs a personal story that isn't in the sources, leave a placeholder for Tarun to fill — don't write one for him.

## Two voices, one personality

| | Company voice ("we") | Founder voice ("I") |
|---|---|---|
| Used on | All service pages, case studies, CTAs, the enquiry flow | About page quotes, keynote descriptions, bylined articles, LinkedIn |
| Reader | CHROs, L&D heads, founders, sales and marketing leaders | The same buyers, plus participants and the community |
| Register | Direct, practical, specific, calm confidence | Warm, energetic, encouraging, plain-spoken |
| Example (real) | "We don't just train individuals — we transform them." (profile p.66) | "I am an IT engineer turned digital marketing consultant and educator who loves spreading smiles." (tarunmakhija.in) |

The corporate site leads with the company voice. The founder voice appears where Tarun himself is the subject.

## Personality — what people actually say about him

From verbatim testimonials (`nextjs/data/testimonials.ts`): *enthusiastic, cheerful, highly skilled*; *"the way he delivers his learnings … is entirely different from the rest"*; *"his passion is what makes him a great teacher"*; participants remember specific techniques ("building audience, consistency") and the energy of the room.

So the voice is: **energetic but grounded, generous, practical, optimistic.** Energy shows in verbs and rhythm, not in exclamation marks or hype words.

## Beliefs he has stated (use these, don't add others)

- Learning should be **practical and enjoyable** — "strategies that are both effective and enjoyable" (tarunmakhija.in).
- Learning should be **relevant, practical, and inspiring** (profile p.2).
- **Success is about impact, not just numbers** (journey article).
- Mission: **empower 1 million individuals by December 1, 2029**. Vision: **help 1 billion people make money by doing what they love** (profile pp.3–4).
- **Personal brand and visibility matter** for professionals and sales teams — "Building trust online in under 3 seconds", the #90DayChallenge (profile p.40).
- **Consistency beats one-off effort** — post-session challenges, review cycles, adoption plans (profile case studies).

## Vocabulary

Use (his and the profile's words): practical, hands-on, real scenarios, capability, team, personal brand, digital-first, confident, high-performing, upskill, growth, consistency, impact, together, workshop, masterclass, learning journey, "Train. Consult. Empower."

Avoid on the corporate site, even though some appear in his older or consumer-facing material:

| Phrase | Where it appears | Why not here |
|---|---|---|
| "unlock your full potential" | old homepage | Generic; no buyer can picture it |
| "top-notch", "unparalleled success" | old homepage | Unsupported superlatives |
| "supercharge", "skyrocket", "dominate" | blog titles | Creator-economy hype; wrong register for CHROs |
| "Book A Call NOW!", "Don't wait, upskill!" | old homepage | Fake urgency; reads as consumer marketing |
| "transform your business", "next level", "cutting-edge", "in today's rapidly evolving world" | — | Could belong to any consultant |
| Emoji in body copy | old homepage ("😊") | Fine on social, not on service pages |

"Captain Tarun" is what MyCaptain students called him — keep it in their quotes, never use it as self-description.

## Sentence habits

- Lead with the person or team doing something: "Your sales team…", "We design…". Not "Solutions are provided…".
- Concrete nouns over abstractions: "4,300+ store staff across 42 cities", not "large-scale enablement".
- Mix sentence lengths. A short sentence after a long one carries the point.
- One idea per sentence on service pages. Two clauses at most.
- Indian/British spelling (organisation, specialise, customisable) — except "program", which the profile and the whole site use. Company figures exactly as published (29,000+, 2,300+).

# B2B conversion copy — TarunSpeaks

`b2b-conversion` decides *which section answers which question*. This skill decides *what the words in that section must say* to a specific buyer. Facts from `nextjs/data/` only.

## Buyers

| Buyer | Problem in their words | Outcome they want | Objections to answer | Decides on |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| CHRO / L&D head | "Training happens, then nothing changes." Teams need AI, sales and leadership skills fast. | Capability that shows up in daily work; something to report upward. | Will it stick? Is it tailored to our roles? Can it scale across cities/cohorts? How much time away from work? | Relevance to roles, delivery format and duration, evidence at comparable scale, follow-through after the session |
| Founder / CEO | "We bought the tools; nobody uses them." Growth stalls on marketing, sales or visibility. | Practical adoption and growth, not theory. | Is this generic? Who actually does the work? Training or real implementation? | Practitioner credibility, specificity, a clear first step |
| Sales / marketing leader | Pipeline, follow-ups, content and personal brand are inconsistent. | A repeatable system the team uses. | Will seniors engage? Does it fit our market? | Peer evidence (NewTech, Motilal Oswal, HDFC Bank), hands-on format |
| Event organiser | Needs a speaker who gives the audience something usable. | An engaged room and a talk people act on. | Topic fit, audience fit, format and length. | Topic catalogue, documented deliveries, easy booking |

## Keep the offers distinct

| Offer | The buyer's question | Say | Don't say |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Corporate training** | "Our people need a new capability." | Modular programs, 3–30 hours; workshop, masterclass or full learning journey; role-specific; documented delivery at scale | "Transformation", promises of ROI |
| **Consulting & AI implementation** | "Leadership needs direction" / "The work has to change." | Advisory areas from the profile; AI tools/agent deployment; adoption plans (Nawany) | "End-to-end transformation partner" |
| **Keynotes & speaking** | "We need a speaker for an event." | Topics, audiences, formats, 100+ keynote sessions, documented venues | Speaking as the whole business |

A CTA must match the offer: training → "Explore Corporate Programs" / "Discuss Your Requirements"; consulting → "Request a Consultation" / "Discuss AI Capability Building"; speaking → "Request a speaker".

## Proof map (strongest evidence per objection)

- *Scale* → Sansaar by D'Decor (4,300+ trained, 42 cities, 100 stores); Motilal Oswal (3,000+, 11 cities); HDFC Bank (1,500+, 7 cities).
- *Will it stick?* → Nawany (personalised AI adoption plan per participant); Motilal Oswal (#90DayChallenge; 500+ first-time LinkedIn posts); NewTech (3-month review cycles).
- *Seniority* → NewTech (senior sales leadership); Godrej Properties (design teams, 2-day intensive).
- *Satisfaction data* → Bentley Systems (96% satisfaction, 100% recommendation) — the only engagement with survey figures; don't generalise them.
- *Breadth* → 29,000+ individuals, 2,300+ sessions, 300+ brands, 15+ industries (profile pp.6–8).

All outcomes are **reported** in the company profile — say so where it matters.

## Never

Invent ROI, revenue impact, adoption percentages, response times, prices, availability or guarantees. Use urgency, scarcity or countdowns. Imply a client endorses TarunSpeaks beyond the documented engagement.

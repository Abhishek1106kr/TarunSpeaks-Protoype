# TARUNSPEAKS — REVENUE-DRIVEN UX & CONVERSION IMPLEMENTATION

## YOUR MISSION

Act as the principal CRO strategist, B2B product designer, customer-journey architect, behavioural UX specialist, senior Next.js engineer, and growth-focused frontend developer for TarunSpeaks.

Your responsibility is to improve the existing website so it generates more qualified business enquiries, consultation bookings, corporate training opportunities, and sales.

This is an implementation task, not a brainstorming exercise.

Inspect the existing website and repository, identify conversion friction, implement the improvements, test them in a real browser, and iterate until the experience is demonstrably better.

Do not stop after producing recommendations, wireframes, or a report.

## 1. BUSINESS OBJECTIVE

Build a website that helps the right visitors move through this journey:

DISCOVER
→ UNDERSTAND VALUE
→ RECOGNISE THEIR PROBLEM
→ FIND THE RIGHT OFFER
→ TRUST TARUNSPEAKS
→ DISCUSS THEIR REQUIREMENTS
→ BUY OR BOOK A CONSULTATION

The commercial objectives are:

1. Increase qualified corporate enquiries.
2. Increase consultation bookings.
3. Increase corporate training and workshop enquiries.
4. Increase interest in AI capability-building and consulting engagements.
5. Help relevant buyers identify the right program without needing to search through the entire website.
6. Reduce confusion, hesitation, and unnecessary steps between interest and enquiry.
7. Improve conversion quality, not merely increase raw form submissions.

Do not promise guaranteed sales growth. Establish a measurable baseline, implement improvements, and instrument the actual customer journey so performance can be evaluated.

## 2. PRIMARY BUYERS

Design primarily for:

- CHROs
- Founders and CEOs
- CXOs and senior leaders
- HR and Learning & Development leaders
- Sales and marketing leaders
- Business leaders evaluating AI adoption, training, and consulting

These buyers have limited time and need to make an informed decision.

They need to understand:

- Is this relevant to my organisation?
- Can TarunSpeaks solve my problem?
- What can I actually engage them for?
- Have they worked with relevant organisations?
- What evidence supports their credibility?
- What does the engagement involve?
- What should I do next?

The website must answer these questions quickly.

Do not optimise the entire experience around students, followers, or casual visitors at the expense of corporate buyers.

## 3. AUDIT THE EXISTING CUSTOMER JOURNEY

Before changing the design, inspect the current website and map the actual conversion journey.

Use Playwright MCP or available browser automation to test the experience as a new visitor.

Inspect:

- Homepage
- Navigation
- Corporate training pages
- Consulting pages
- Program and course pages
- Case studies
- Client and industry sections
- Speaking pages
- Contact and enquiry forms
- Existing booking integrations
- Mobile layouts
- CTA destinations
- Form validation
- Loading and success states
- Analytics and tracking integrations, if present

Identify friction such as:

- Unclear value proposition
- Weak or generic headlines
- No obvious next step
- Too many competing CTAs
- Service descriptions without clear buyer benefits
- Missing program details
- Credibility buried too far down the page
- Case studies without a clear problem and outcome
- Confusing navigation
- Excessive scrolling before a relevant offer appears
- Forms that ask too much too soon
- Broken links or dead-end pages
- Poor mobile interaction
- Fake buttons or non-functional CTAs
- Repetitive generic cards
- Slow or distracting animations

Produce an internal list of the highest-impact problems and fix them directly.

Prioritise by likely commercial impact, confidence, and implementation effort.

Do not spend the entire task analysing. Move from diagnosis to implementation.

## 4. BUILD TWO CLEAR BUYER PATHS

The website should help different buyers find their route without creating two disconnected brands.

### Path A: CHROs and organisational leaders

Their likely needs include corporate training, workforce AI capability, workshops, leadership development, sales capability, marketing capability, and organisational implementation.

Build a clear journey:

ORGANISATIONAL CHALLENGE
→ RELEVANT PROGRAM
→ DELIVERY APPROACH
→ RELEVANT CLIENT EVIDENCE
→ DISCUSS REQUIREMENTS
→ CONSULTATION OR ENQUIRY

Use real services and approved evidence.

### Path B: Founders and business leaders

Their likely needs include practical AI adoption, business productivity, digital marketing capability, consulting, and support with implementation.

Build a journey:

BUSINESS CHALLENGE
→ RELEVANT SERVICE
→ PRACTICAL VALUE
→ PROOF AND EXPERIENCE
→ DISCUSS THE BUSINESS
→ CONSULTATION OR ENQUIRY

Do not assume every founder has the same needs.

Allow buyers to choose a relevant route through clear labels, service navigation, and contextual CTAs.

Keep the main navigation concise.

## 5. REBUILD THE HOMEPAGE AROUND CONVERSION

The homepage should not be a long visual portfolio. It should guide visitors toward a relevant decision.

### Section 1: Immediate value proposition

The first viewport must clearly establish:

- What TarunSpeaks does
- Who it serves
- What business problems it addresses
- What action the visitor can take

Use an approved, specific value proposition related to corporate training, consulting, and AI capability-building.

Avoid generic claims such as "Unlock your potential" or "Transform the future" unless they are part of approved brand copy and explain a specific benefit.

Primary CTA: **Talk to TarunSpeaks**

Secondary CTA: **Explore Programs**

The primary CTA must lead to a real, usable enquiry or booking experience.

### Section 2: Credibility

Present approved company evidence prominently:

- 29,000+ individuals trained
- 2,300+ sessions
- 300+ brands
- 15+ industries

Verify wording and context against the company profile.

Make these figures readable and meaningful. Do not rely on animations to reveal them.

### Section 3: Choose your objective

Help visitors self-identify through a small number of clear choices, such as:

- Build AI capability across my organisation
- Improve sales or marketing capability
- Explore corporate training and workshops
- Discuss consulting or implementation
- Book a keynote or speaking engagement

Only include choices supported by actual TarunSpeaks offerings.

Each choice should lead to a relevant page or preselect the appropriate enquiry category.

Do not create fake diagnostic quizzes or meaningless interactive selectors.

### Section 4: Programs and solutions

Organise actual services by buyer problem and desired capability.

For every offer, communicate the relevant information that is available:

- Who it is for
- The problem it addresses
- What is included
- Delivery format
- Duration, where verified
- What participants will learn or implement
- Relevant evidence
- Next step

Avoid vague descriptions that force visitors to contact the company just to discover what a service actually includes.

Do not invent prices, guarantees, durations, or results.

### Section 5: Evidence and case studies

Use the most relevant documented client engagements.

Structure case studies around:

CLIENT
→ BUSINESS CHALLENGE
→ WHAT TARUNSPEAKS DELIVERED
→ DOCUMENTED OUTCOME
→ RELATED SERVICE
→ ENQUIRY CTA

Choose the evidence that best matches the buyer's likely problem.

Do not manufacture results, testimonials, ROI, or client endorsements.

### Section 6: How an engagement works

Explain the real process clearly.

Where supported by the actual delivery approach, show:

DISCOVERY
→ PROGRAM DESIGN
→ TRAINING OR CONSULTING
→ PRACTICAL APPLICATION
→ FOLLOW-UP OR IMPLEMENTATION SUPPORT

Adjust the sequence to reflect actual TarunSpeaks engagements.

Reduce uncertainty by explaining what happens after someone enquires.

### Section 7: Client ecosystem

Show the 300+ brands and 15+ industries positioning with an attractive, accessible client-logo experience.

Use the supplied industry categories:

- BFSI, Real Estate, Furnishings & Logistics
- Education & Training
- Retail, FMCG, Media & Entertainment
- Others

Use verified logos where available. Preserve the supplied client names and groupings.

Let visitors explore the organisations without turning the homepage into an enormous directory.

### Section 8: Tarun's authority

Use Tarun's real experience, journey, photographs, and expertise to reinforce trust.

Position Tarun as the practitioner behind the work.

The business value and credibility must remain more prominent than celebrity-style presentation.

### Section 9: Frequently asked questions

Use actual recurring buyer questions where available.

Potential subjects include:

- Who are the programs designed for?
- Can an engagement be tailored to our organisation?
- What delivery formats are available?
- How do we discuss our requirements?
- What happens after we submit an enquiry?

Answer from verified business information. Do not invent policies, promises, or commitments.

### Section 10: Conversion-focused close

End with a clear invitation for qualified buyers.

Example direction:

"Let's discuss the capability your organisation needs to build."

CTA: **Discuss Your Requirements**

The CTA must lead to a working next step.

## 6. DESIGN THE ENTIRE CONVERSION EXPERIENCE

Do not limit improvements to the homepage.

Review the routes buyers actually visit before contacting the company.

### Service pages

Every service page should answer:

1. Who is this for?
2. What problem does it address?
3. What does the engagement include?
4. How is it delivered?
5. What evidence supports its relevance?
6. What happens after I enquire?

Place contextual CTAs where a buyer has enough information to act.

Do not repeat a generic CTA after every paragraph.

### Case-study pages

Connect each case study to the service it demonstrates.

Include a relevant next step, such as discussing a similar organisational challenge.

Use only verified information.

### Course pages

Preserve existing course information, pricing, policies, and working purchase flows.

Do not change prices or payment behaviour without authorisation.

Differentiate individual course purchases from corporate engagement enquiries.

### Speaking pages

Provide a clear topic and format catalogue, using verified information.

Make it easy for a corporate organiser to enquire about a suitable engagement.

Do not allow speaking to overshadow the broader training and consulting business.

## 7. CREATE A LOW-FRICTION ENQUIRY SYSTEM

Inspect the existing forms and integrations before implementing anything new.

Use an appropriate form for each intent:

- Corporate training enquiry
- Consulting enquiry
- AI capability-building enquiry
- Speaking enquiry
- General contact

Avoid building a separate form for every minor variation if a single well-designed form can handle the request clearly.

### Initial enquiry fields

Prefer a short initial form with fields such as:

- Name
- Work email
- Organisation
- Role, if useful
- Area of interest
- Brief description of the challenge

Use progressive disclosure for optional details.

Ask for a phone number, budget, timeline, or other sensitive business information only when it is genuinely necessary for the relevant workflow.

### Form behaviour

Implement:

- Clear labels
- Helpful validation
- Inline error messages
- Keyboard accessibility
- Loading states
- Duplicate-submission protection
- A real success state
- A useful recovery path if submission fails
- Spam protection appropriate to the existing infrastructure

Use the existing backend or form provider when available.

Do not display a success message unless the submission has actually succeeded.

Never expose credentials in frontend code.

### After submission

Give the visitor a clear explanation of what happens next, based only on the actual business process.

If a calendar booking integration exists, make it available where appropriate.

If it does not exist, do not fabricate a booking system or promise a response time.

## 8. CONSULTATION BOOKING

Make consultation booking easy for visitors who are ready to talk.

Inspect existing booking tools, links, CRM connections, and calendar integrations.

If a working booking destination exists:

- Use it in relevant CTAs.
- Make the booking experience accessible on mobile.
- Preserve useful context about the visitor's selected interest.
- Avoid unnecessary redirects or duplicate data entry.

If no booking integration exists:

- Implement a clear enquiry path using the existing infrastructure.
- Keep the code integration-ready.
- Document what authorisation or connection is still needed.

Do not invent calendar availability, meeting slots, or successful bookings.

Distinguish between a general corporate enquiry and a request for a consultation when that distinction helps the business qualify the lead.

## 9. TRUST AND BUYER CONFIDENCE

Trust is a conversion requirement, not a decorative section.

Use:

- Verified client organisations
- Real corporate engagement photographs
- Approved case studies
- Accurate program descriptions
- Real testimonials with permission
- Transparent engagement information
- Clear contact options
- Consistent branding
- Accessible design
- Reliable form behaviour
- Honest descriptions of outcomes

Do not add fake review stars, invented testimonials, fabricated logos, false urgency, countdown timers, manipulative pop-ups, or unsupported guarantees.

Do not use dark patterns.

The goal is to make a good purchasing decision easier, not to pressure visitors into contacting the company.

## 10. VISUAL DESIGN AND UX

Retain the approved black and rich blue direction:

- Black: `#080B10`
- Deep surface: `#0D121B`
- Elevated surface: `#131B28`
- Rich blue: `#1749D1`
- Bright blue: `#2864F0`
- Primary text: `#F5F7FB`
- Secondary text: `#AAB5C7`

Improve the typography, layout, spacing, photography, information hierarchy, and visual rhythm.

Avoid generic AI-generated aesthetics, repetitive cards, unnecessary gradients, excessive glassmorphism, glowing borders, and meaningless motion.

The experience should feel premium, editorial, human, executive-grade, and commercially clear.

Use animation only where it improves comprehension or makes a meaningful interaction feel better.

Make mobile UX a first-class design requirement.

## 11. ANALYTICS AND CONVERSION MEASUREMENT

Inspect the existing analytics implementation before introducing new tracking.

Where a suitable analytics system exists, instrument meaningful events such as:

- `primary_cta_clicked`
- `program_viewed`
- `case_study_viewed`
- `enquiry_started`
- `enquiry_submitted`
- `consultation_booking_started`
- `consultation_booked`, only when a booking is actually confirmed

Include useful context such as the route, service category, CTA location, and campaign attribution when available and appropriate.

Avoid collecting unnecessary personal information in analytics events.

Use existing analytics conventions and respect applicable consent and privacy requirements.

Do not install multiple analytics systems unnecessarily.

If analytics is not connected, prepare a clean event interface and document the integration required. Do not claim that events are being tracked when they are not.

### Measurement

Establish a baseline if data is available.

Track:

- Qualified enquiry conversion rate
- Consultation booking completion rate
- Form abandonment
- CTA engagement
- Service-page engagement
- Lead quality
- Enquiry-to-consultation progression, if the relevant CRM data is available

Do not use raw button clicks as a substitute for sales.

Do not claim that conversion has improved until actual comparative data supports the conclusion.

A founder should quickly find relevant business, AI, and consulting services.

Both should see credible evidence, understand the next step, and be able to complete an enquiry without unnecessary friction.

Every primary CTA must have a real destination. Every form must have a genuine submission flow. Every claimed integration must actually work.

Implement the improvements in the existing repository, verify them in a real browser, and report the actual results.
